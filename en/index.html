Monumental Artist

George Klapsa Gogolashvili

Georgian Artistic Heritage in Contemporary Monumental Painting

George Klapsa Gogolashvili is a Georgian monumental artist whose practice brings together the traditions of Georgian ecclesiastical painting, academic training, and the expressive possibilities of architectural space.

Educated in Tbilisi and professionally established in New York, Gogolashvili has developed a body of work that reflects a sustained engagement with historical painting techniques, sacred iconography, and large-scale decorative composition.

Among his most significant documented projects are the monumental paintings created for the Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection in Manhattan, where elements of Georgia's medieval artistic heritage find expression within an American Orthodox setting.

About the Artist

From Tbilisi to New York

Born and raised in the Vake district of Tbilisi, Georgia, George Klapsa Gogolashvili developed his artistic identity within a cultural environment shaped by painting, theatre, and the traditions of monumental art.

Areas of practice

Sacred and Ecclesiastical Painting

Monumental compositions informed by Christian iconography, historical sources, and the architectural character of sacred spaces.

Monumental and Decorative Art

An approach that brings painting, spatial composition, and architectural decoration into dialogue.

Historical Techniques and Restoration Practice

An ongoing interest in traditional artistic methods, historical painting, and the study of earlier masters.
