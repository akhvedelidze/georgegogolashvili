Press and media

Interviews, programs, and publications

Georgian television and the press have covered George (Klapsa) Gogolashvili's work and the murals he painted in New York. Interviews, programs, and publications are gathered on this page.

They include the Sarke interview, Georgian Public Broadcaster's My Story, Uechveli Kartvelia, Akhali 9, and Homecoming for the Future. The certificate of recognition from the Orthodox Church in America, for the creation and installation of the archangel murals, is here as well.
