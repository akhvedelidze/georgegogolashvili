Works

Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection

Location
59 East 2nd Street, Manhattan, New York
Discipline
Sacred monumental painting

The paintings created for the Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection represent a significant chapter in Gogolashvili's artistic career.

Within the historic architectural setting of the cathedral, he developed monumental compositions informed by Christian iconography and the traditions of medieval Georgian painting.

The cathedral building was constructed in 1891. It stands at 59 East 2nd Street in Manhattan.

The project is distinguished by its dialogue between historical artistic sources and the architecture of an American Orthodox cathedral.

Among its principal documented works are The Mystical Supper and the sanctuary paintings depicting archangels and angelic hosts.
