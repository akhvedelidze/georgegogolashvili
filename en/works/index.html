Selected Works and Projects

George Klapsa Gogolashvili's documented practice encompasses monumental painting, sacred imagery, architectural decoration, and work involving historical artistic techniques.

This collection introduces selected projects through their visual composition, architectural setting, and relationship to Georgian artistic heritage.

The Ascension

The Ascension is a monumental composition by George Klapsa Gogolashvili on the ascent of Christ. The Virgin stands at the center, the apostles look upward, and the ascending Christ appears above them.

View the work

The Nativity

The Nativity is a monumental composition by George Klapsa Gogolashvili. The photograph shows the Virgin, the child, a shepherd, and an angel, while the adjoining wall still carries only an outline.
