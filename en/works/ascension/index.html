Works

The Ascension

Discipline
Monumental sacred composition

The Ascension is a monumental composition by George (Klapsa) Gogolashvili on the ascent of Christ. The Virgin stands at the center, the apostles look upward, and the ascending Christ appears above them.

The photographs record the structure of the composition and the handling of the figures. In some frames the lower ground is still unfinished. The final architectural setting and completion date are not established in the materials supplied for this site.
