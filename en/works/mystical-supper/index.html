Works

The Mystical Supper

Location
Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection, New York
Discipline
Ecclesiastical wall painting
Dates
2011–2012, documented working period

The Mystical Supper is one of the central works in George (Klapsa) Gogolashvili's documented practice of monumental sacred painting.

During the development of the composition, the artist studied Georgian medieval fresco painting, focusing particularly on works associated with the fourteenth-century painter Damiane at Ubisi Monastery.

According to Gogolashvili's account, the work involved preparatory compositional studies followed by a traditional method of transferring the design directly onto the wall using charcoal.

The resulting project demonstrates an approach in which historical iconographic references are interpreted for a new architectural setting, rather than treated merely as decorative motifs.

The artist has stated that he donated the completed work to the cathedral.

The years 2011–2012 record a documented period of work on this composition, not an officially established completion date. The photographs shown here record printed images of the composition.
