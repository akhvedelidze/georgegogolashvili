Works

Archangels and the Angelic Hosts

Location
Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection, New York
Discipline
Sacred monumental painting
Dates
2018 / 2019

The sanctuary paintings depicting archangels and angelic hosts form an important component of Gogolashvili's work at the Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection.

The artist has identified the fresco tradition of Gelati Monastery as a significant source of inspiration for these compositions.

The paintings engage with the visual language of Orthodox iconography while responding to the spatial and liturgical character of the sanctuary.

In a 2022 interview, Gogolashvili described the sanctuary paintings as completed in 2018.

On October 13, 2019, he received an Orthodox Church in America certificate of recognition for the creation and installation of the murals of the Holy Choir of Archangels at the Cathedral of the Holy Virgin Protection. The certificate is signed by Archimandrite Christopher, Rector.
