The Nativity

Discipline
Monumental sacred composition

The Nativity is a monumental composition by George (Klapsa) Gogolashvili. The photograph shows the Virgin, the child, a shepherd, and an angel, while the adjoining wall still carries only an outline.

A preparatory drawing is also preserved. The completion date and final setting are not established in the materials supplied for this site.
